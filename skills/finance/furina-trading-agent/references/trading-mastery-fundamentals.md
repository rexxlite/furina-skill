# 10 Fundamental Trading Mastery Topics

Compiled 2026-05-29 for Furina's trading education reference. Serves as the knowledge base for improving scanner logic and understanding market behavior.

## 1. Market Structure & Price Action
- **Foundation of all TA** — HH/HL = uptrend, LH/LL = downtrend
- **Break of Structure (BOS)** = trend continuation signal
- **Change of Character (CHoCH)** = early reversal signal
- Pitfall: jumping to indicators without reading structure first

## 2. Support & Resistance / Supply & Demand
- **Support** = floor (buyer defend), **Resistance** = ceiling (seller defend)
- **Supply zone** = area where sellers dominate, **Demand zone** = buyer dominance
- Role reversal: broken support → resistance, broken resistance → support
- Focus on SIGNIFICANT levels (swing high/low major), not over-drawing

## 3. Candlestick Patterns
- Single: Doji, Hammer, Shooting Star, Engulfing
- Double: Bullish/Bearish Engulfing, Harami
- Triple: Morning Star, Evening Star, Three White Soldiers
- **Context matters!** Hammer at resistance ≠ hammer at support
- Use as CONFIRMATION only, not standalone entry trigger

## 4. Technical Indicators (RSI, MACD, MA, BB)
- **RSI**: momentum (0-100), >70 overbought, <30 oversold, divergence is key
- **MACD**: trend + momentum, signal line crossover, histogram for strength
- **EMA 20/50/200**: dynamic S/R, Golden/Death Cross
- **Bollinger Bands**: volatility measure, squeeze → breakout coming, band walk = strong trend
- Pitfall: stacking too many indicators (2-3 max)

## 5. Volume Analysis
- Volume confirms trend: price up + volume up = healthy trend
- Volume divergence: price up + volume down = weakening
- Volume climax/spike = potential reversal
- Low volume breakout = likely fake/bull trap
- **Accumulation** = smart money buying quietly (volume up, price sideways)
- **Distribution** = smart money selling quietly

## 6. Chart Patterns
- **Continuation**: Flag, Pennant, Ascending/Descending Triangle, Cup & Handle
- **Reversal**: Head & Shoulders (most reliable), Double Top/Bottom, Triple Top/Bottom
- Wait for CONFIRMATION (breakout neckline + volume), don't anticipate
- Pitfall: forcing patterns that aren't there

## 7. Risk Management & Position Sizing
- Risk per trade: max 1-2% of total capital
- Formula: `Size = (Capital × Risk%) / (Entry - SL)`
- Min RR: 1:1.5, ideal 1:2+
- Max drawdown limit: stop trading if DD > 10-15%
- **Fact**: profitable with 40% winrate IF RR is 1:2+
- Pitfall: averaging down without plan, no SL, oversized positions

## 8. Trading Psychology
- **FOMO**: entry without plan from fear of missing out
- **Revenge Trading**: doubling down after loss → bigger losses
- **Greed**: not taking TP because "it'll go higher"
- **Fear**: SL too close from fear of big loss
- **Overtrading**: too many entries from boredom
- Solution: written trading plan, journal all trades, accept loss as cost of business

## 9. Order Flow & Market Microstructure
- Bid/Ask spread, Order book depth
- Smart money vs retail: institutions have info + speed advantage
- **Liquidity pools**: areas where SLs cluster (become "hunt" targets)
- **Stop hunt**: price "chasing" SLs before moving to intended direction
- **Iceberg orders**: large orders split to hide size
- Market is NOT random — big players move price toward liquidity

## 10. Multi-Timeframe Analysis (MTF) & Confluence
- **HTF** (Weekly/Daily): trend direction & key levels
- **MTF** (4H/1H): structure & setup zone
- **LTF** (15m/5m): entry trigger
- **Golden rule**: trade WITH HTF trend
- **Confluence zone**: area where 2+ signals meet (S/R + fib + EMA + demand zone)
- More confluence = higher probability
- Pitfall: trading 15m without checking Daily = often counter-trend
